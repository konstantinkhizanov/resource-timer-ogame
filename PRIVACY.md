# Privacy Policy — Resource Timer for OGame

Resource Timer for OGame does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The extension only runs on OGame game pages (`*.ogame.gameforge.com/game/*`). It reads the resource amounts and build costs already shown on the page and calculates waiting times locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. No analytics, no tracking, no cookies.

Contact: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
